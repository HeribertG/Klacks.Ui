Overtime

These settings define when extra work counts as overtime and at what supplement it is paid. Three tiers allow graduated supplements to be modelled. The values act as a company-wide default.

Calculation basis

Reference period against which the hour thresholds of the tiers are checked.

  • Daily: overtime is determined per working day.
  • Weekly: overtime is determined per week.

Rate mode

Determines how the supplement value of the tiers is interpreted.

  • Multiplier: factor on the hourly wage. The supplement is entered as a percentage (e.g. 25 % means a factor of 1.25, i.e. a 25 % uplift).
  • Fixed amount per hour: a fixed amount per overtime hour instead of a percentage factor.

Tiers 1 to 3

Each tier consists of an hour threshold and a supplement. The tier applies as soon as the hours worked exceed the threshold within the chosen reference period. Higher tiers model additional thresholds for higher supplements.

  • After hours: threshold in hours from which the tier applies.
  • Supplement (%): amount of the supplement for this tier, entered as a percentage.
  • Leave unused tiers empty; they are then not applied.
