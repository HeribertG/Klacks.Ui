Active industries

Klacks ships preset content for five industries: home care, healthcare, security, facility services and logistics. This card controls which of these industries are considered active for your company.

What the selection affects

Only presets of active industries (for example scheduling rule presets and qualification presets) appear in selection lists throughout the application. Deactivating an industry only filters these lists.

  • Presets of deactivated industries are kept — nothing is deleted.
  • Re-activating an industry makes its presets visible again immediately.

Default

When the setting has never been saved, all five industries are active.

Validation

At least one industry must remain active. An empty selection is not saved; the card shows a hint until at least one industry is selected again.
