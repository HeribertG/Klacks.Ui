Restricted Time Windows – Manual

What are restricted time windows?

A restricted time window forbids scheduling shifts within a certain part of the day during a certain season. This can be used, for example, to model a night-scheduling ban for part of the year or industry-specific blackout periods.

The settings in detail

Season from (month / day)

Start of the season in which the window applies, as month (1–12) and day (1–31). The season refers to the shift's date, not to a calendar year, and may span the turn of the year (see below).

Season to (month / day)

End of the season as month and day. If the end lies before the start (e.g. from 1 November to 28 February), the season applies across the turn of the year.

Daily window – start / end

The time of day during which the restriction applies, as a start time and end time (HH:mm). A window whose end time lies before the start time (e.g. 22:00 to 06:00) applies across midnight.

Applies to group tag

Restricts the block to shifts of a specific group (via its tag/identifier). If the field is left empty, the restriction applies to all shifts.

Important note on imported rules

  • Rules from a region setup (country profile) are restored when the region setup is re-imported. Deleting such a row is therefore not permanent – it reappears after the next re-import.
