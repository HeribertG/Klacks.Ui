Holiday Rules - Explanations

Rule Format

There are two types of rules: fixed dates and Easter-related dates.

Fixed Dates

Format: MM/DD

ExampleMeaning
01/01January 1st (New Year's Day)
12/25December 25th (Christmas)
07/04July 4th (Independence Day)

Fixed Dates with Weekday Offset

Format: MM/DD+XX+WW or MM/DD+XX-WW

  • +XX = Sign + offset in days (00 to 99)
  • + = next weekday after the date
  • - = previous weekday before the date
  • WW = Weekday (SU, MO, TU, WE, TH, FR, SA)
ExampleMeaning
11/01+00+THFirst Thursday in November
11/22+00+THFourth Thursday in November (Thanksgiving)
05/25+00-MOLast Monday in May (Memorial Day)

Easter-Related Dates

Format: EASTER+XX or EASTER-XX

ExampleMeaning
EASTER+00Easter Sunday
EASTER-02Good Friday (2 days before Easter)
EASTER+01Easter Monday (1 day after Easter)
EASTER+39Ascension Day (39 days after Easter)
EASTER+49Whit Sunday/Pentecost (49 days after Easter)
EASTER+50Whit Monday (50 days after Easter)
EASTER+60Corpus Christi (60 days after Easter)

SubRule Format

SubRules allow automatic shifting of a holiday when it falls on a specific weekday.

Format: WW+X or WW-X, multiple rules separated by ;

  • WW = Weekday (SU, MO, TU, WE, TH, FR, SA)
  • +X = Shift X days forward
  • -X = Shift X days backward
ExampleMeaning
SA+2;SU+1If Saturday: +2 days (Monday), if Sunday: +1 day (Monday)
SU+1If Sunday: shift to Monday

Note: SubRules are typically used to shift holidays that fall on weekends to the next business day.
