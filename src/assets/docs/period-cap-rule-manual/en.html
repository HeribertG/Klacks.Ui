Period Caps – Manual

What are period caps?

Period caps limit a person's working time over a defined period. They support two modes: a fixed cap for a calendar period (e.g. maximum hours per month) and a rolling window with a maximum average of weekly hours over several past weeks.

The settings in detail

Period

The calendar period the fixed cap refers to:

  • Month, quarter or year – the respective calendar period.
  • Custom weeks – a freely chosen period in weeks (see period weeks).

Scope

Which hours are counted:

  • Total hours – all scheduled working hours.
  • Overtime hours – only the hours reported as overtime.

Cap hours

The maximum permitted number of hours within the chosen period and scope. Once the value is reached or exceeded, the schedule reports it.

Warn at percent (%)

Percentage of the cap at which an early warning should be raised (e.g. 80%). Reserved: this value is not yet evaluated and serves as a placeholder for a future early-warning stage.

Period weeks

Only relevant and only shown when the period custom weeks is selected. Length of the period in weeks; 1 to 104 weeks are permitted.

Rolling window weeks

Length of the look-back rolling window in weeks. Together with the maximum average weekly hours this activates the rolling-window mode: the average over the last n weeks is checked.

Max. average weekly hours

The highest permitted average of working hours per week across the rolling window. Example: 48 hours with a rolling window of 17 weeks limits the rolling weekly average to 48 hours.

Scheduling rule binding (optional)

Links the period cap to an existing scheduling rule. Left empty, the cap applies on its own.

Important note on imported rules

  • Rules from a region setup (country profile) are restored when the region setup is re-imported. Deleting such a row is therefore not permanent – it reappears after the next re-import.
  • A scheduling rule binding set on an imported row is reset to its original value on re-import. Manually created rules are not affected.
