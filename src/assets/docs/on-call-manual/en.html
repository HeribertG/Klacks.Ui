On-call and standby duty

These settings define whether on-call and standby duties are tracked and what share of the time spent counts as working time. They act as a company-wide default for newly planned duties.

On-call enabled

Turns the whole on-call logic on or off. When disabled, the remaining fields are ignored and presence and standby time are not counted separately.

  • Default: disabled.

Presence duty counts as (%)

Share of active on-site presence time that is credited as regular working time.

  • Range: 0 to 100 percent.
  • Default: 100 (presence time counts fully as working time).

Standby counts as (%)

Share of pure standby time (on-call without an active assignment) that is credited as working time.

  • Range: 0 to 100 percent.
  • Default: 0 (standby does not count as working time).

Include in period caps

Determines whether the credited on-call and standby time is included in the period caps (e.g. monthly or yearly maximum hours).

  • Enabled: credited time counts against the period caps.
  • Default: disabled.
